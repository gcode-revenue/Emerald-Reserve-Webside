Smart Living

Technology, crafted like jewelry

Every Emerald Reserve residence can be prepared for integrated smart-home control. Lighting, shades, climate, security and the front door respond to a touch, a turn of a faceted ring, or your phone from anywhere in the world.

One System, Every Room

The house adjusts itself to the hour, the weather and the way you live, and stays out of sight until you need it.

HyPanel Lux · On the Wall

The control panel you want to be seen

A round touch screen sits inside a diamond-faceted metal ring, circled by a soft light ring that can glow in any color. Turn the ring to dim the lights or warm their tone. Tap the screen to open the shades to the lake. Press a glass button for Sunset, Dinner or All Off.

HyPanel Lux Vase · On the Table

Cut crystal, with the whole house inside

The same round screen, set on a faceted crystal base. It needs no wall wiring, so it can rest on a nightstand, a vanity or the kitchen island. The base glows softly with ambient light, and a built-in intercom reaches other rooms and the front door.

At the Front Door

See who's there from any room

When a guest arrives, the panels and your phone show who is at the door, so you can speak with them and let them in from the terrace, the suite or another country.

An Arrive scene greets you at night with the staircase and entry lights already on. Away turns everything off, sets back the climate and arms security as you leave.

Scenes

A day at Emerald Reserve

A scene sets lights, shades, climate and security in one action. These are examples. Each owner's scenes are planned with them during design.

  1. 7:00 AM

    Lake Light

    Shades rise on the lake side and the kitchen lights come up softly.

  2. 9:00 AM

    Away

    Lights off, climate set back, doors locked and security armed.

  3. 6:45 PM

    Sunset

    Warm, dimmed light inside and the garden lights on outside.

  4. 11:00 PM

    Good Night

    The house goes dark, the shutters close and the perimeter is armed.

  5. Storm Watch

    Storm Ready

    Every automated shutter closes together ahead of a tropical storm.
