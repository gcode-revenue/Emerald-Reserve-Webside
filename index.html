A One-of-a-Kind Setting

Unparalleled privacy on the water

Emerald Reserve is a private community with direct lake access, surrounded by a natural preserve. Set among rolling tropical hills, every residence is positioned to capture sweeping water views, offering unparalleled privacy, natural beauty and an elevated lifestyle in Puerto Rico.

With only ten homesites, the community is intentionally intimate — a rare opportunity to own a lakefront estate in one of the island's most serene landscapes.
