The Project

Ten residences.
One extraordinary lake.

Emerald Reserve is a private lakefront community in Puerto Rico, conceived for those who value privacy, nature and an elevated way of living.

Overview

A natural reserve, reimagined as home

Nestled along the shore of a tranquil lake and embraced by lush, protected forest, Emerald Reserve offers just ten exclusive residences in a one-of-a-kind setting.

The community combines direct lake access with the seclusion of a natural preserve. Homes are sited to take full advantage of the landscape — every residence enjoys water views, and select waterfront homesites include a private dock.

  • Private lakefront community with direct lake access
  • Only 10 unique properties
  • Approximately 5,500 sq. ft. of construction per residence
  • Direct lake views from every room

Architecture

Timeless Mediterranean design

Clay-tile roofs, soft cream façades, arched entries and expansive steel-framed windows define the architecture of Emerald Reserve. Grand staircases lead to covered loggias, while landscaped motor courts and tropical gardens create a warm sense of arrival.

Inside, open living spaces flow out to terraces and heated infinity pools, bringing the lake into everyday life.

Two paths to ownership

  • Custom homes — design a residence tailored entirely to your vision.
  • Pre-finished, built-to-suit — a refined home ready for your personal touches.
